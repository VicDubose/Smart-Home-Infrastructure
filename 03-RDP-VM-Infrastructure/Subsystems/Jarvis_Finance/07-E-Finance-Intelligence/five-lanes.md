# Five Finance Intelligence Lanes

These are the intended consumer capabilities. Their definition does not claim that all five are implemented or continuously operating.

| Lane | Question | Required evidence | Intended output |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 — Market Intelligence | What changed publicly? | Qualified public events, period-consistent indices and rates | Changes, provenance, uncertainty, expiration |
| F2 — Debt Strategy | What repayment action is affordable? | Verified balances, APR types, due dates, income, protected reserves | Scenario comparisons and contractual-risk flags |
| F3 — Future Allocation | Where should verified available funds go next? | Cash headroom, obligations, goals, constraints | Bounded alternatives and tradeoffs |
| F4 — Portfolio Opportunities | Which opportunities merit research or monitoring? | Verified private exposure plus separately labeled market context | Research / hold / rebalance / avoid candidates, never orders |
| F5 — Financial Health | Can the current plan work? | Posted income, spending, bills, debt, reserves | Trends, forecast risks, missing-evidence flags |

## Cross-lane arbitration

An attractive market opportunity can still be unaffordable. Debt, reserve, and investment lanes must not issue conflicting spending instructions. A future capital arbiter may compare validated priorities against approved policy and liquidity, but that runtime is a design goal until separately accepted.

A decision journal and paper portfolio are also future capabilities, not accepted live services.
